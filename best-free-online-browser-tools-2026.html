Why Browser-Based Tools?

Browser-based tools use JavaScript to process data locally on your device. When you drag an image or paste text into one of these tools, everything happens inside your browser's memory. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, nothing is tracked. This means:

  • Privacy: Your sensitive files never reach a server
  • Speed: No network latency — processing is instant
  • Offline: Many tools work without internet after first load
  • Free: No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no credit cards
