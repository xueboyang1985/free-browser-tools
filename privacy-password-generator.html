Common Questions

Can this website see my passwords?

No. The JavaScript runs entirely in your browser. Open DevTools → Network tab while generating passwords — you will see zero network requests.

Why is crypto.getRandomValues better than Math.random?

Math.random() is predictable — someone who knows the seed can recreate your passwords. crypto.getRandomValues() uses your operating system's entropy source, which is cryptographically secure.
